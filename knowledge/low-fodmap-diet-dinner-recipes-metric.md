Preheat oven to 375ºF (191ºC).
Add the quinoa and water to a sauce pot. Place over high heat and bring to a
boil. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cover with a lid. Let simmer for 12

baking dish and repeat until all the leaves have been rolled.

and bake for 40 minutes. Remove the lid or foil and bake for an additional 10
minutes uncovered.
