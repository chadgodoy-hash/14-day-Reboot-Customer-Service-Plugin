Emulsifiers  and  Gums  
There  is  some  confusion  surrounding  carrageenan,  xanthan  gum  an  guar  gum,  which  are  common  food  
additives.
 
These  are  all  low  FODMAP  and  permitted  on  a  low  FODMAP  diet  in  moderate  quantities.  
However,  some  do  not  absorb  the  gums  well  and  they  can  cause  digestive  stress  in  select  people  (but  it's  
unrelated
 
to
 
FODMAPs
 
specifically).
 
So  they  are  permitted,  but  keep  that  in  mind  if  you  are  having  symptoms  despite  a  strict  low  FODMAP  
diet.
