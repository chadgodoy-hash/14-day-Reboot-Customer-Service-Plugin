## Page 2

This is a sample meal plan that provides you with a healthy
eating pattern. 
IIt's designed to be very basic and simple to follow. It's also
high in foods naturally rich in L-methylfolate (a different
structure than folic acid). 
I've also included lactose-free and gluten-free
recommendations as these are generally better tolerated. 
Aim to drink water, black coffee and black or herbal tea as
your main drinks. 
For a more detailed explanation about the diet
recommendations please see the MTHFR 101 Roadmap.
